1. Acceptance of Terms and Educational Engagement Charter

2. Intellectual Property Rights and Scholar Research Autonomy

AxiomLearnCrest champions the paramount intellectual independence and creative autonomy of its research fellows, maintaining that genuine scholarly innovation thrives only when creators retain ownership of their discoveries. All original mathematical proofs, algorithmic formulations, scientific monographs, and computational software architectures conceived, authored, or implemented solely by fellows during their tenure remain the exclusive intellectual property of the respective scholar, subject only to a non-exclusive archival license granted to the institute for educational repository preservation. Conversely, all proprietary course syllabi, lecture transcriptions, seminar recordings, and specialized pedagogical methodologies developed by AxiomLearnCrest faculty remain the protected intellectual property of the institute and may not be redistributed, sold, or reproduced without prior written consent.

3. Academic Integrity, Originality of Proofs, and Ethical Conduct

The academic reputation of AxiomLearnCrest rests entirely upon the uncompromising integrity, honesty, and rigor of its scholarly community, necessitating absolute adherence to ethical research practices. All solutions to admissions problem sets, seminar assignments, and fellowship treatises must represent the authentic, unassisted intellectual labor of the submitting scholar, with all external theorems, bibliographic citations, and collaborative peer discussions meticulously and transparently documented. Any documented instance of academic dishonesty, including automated theorem plagiarism, falsification of computational experiment outputs, or misrepresentation of academic credentials, will result in immediate termination of fellowship appointments, revocation of institutional honors, and permanent expulsion from all current and future academy seminars.

4. Compute Resource Allocation and Responsible Computational Usage

Fellows granted access credentials to the Turing Compute Cluster and remote astrophysical observatories must utilize these high-performance resources strictly for approved academic research, algorithm validation, and formal theorem proving. Scholars are strictly forbidden from utilizing institute computational infrastructure for cryptocurrency mining, unauthorized commercial workloads, external penetration testing, or the automated distribution of unsolicited communications across public networks. AxiomLearnCrest reserves the unilateral right to audit resource consumption metrics, terminate runaway processes, and suspend or revoke compute privileges for any individual who violates these responsible computing protocols or whose resource consumption endangers the stability of our academic research grid.
